❖ Methodology:-
Screen
Capture
🔍
Captures the live webpage screen inside the browser. Scans visible buttons, links, & text inputs. Tags each interactive item with normalized coordinates.
On-Device
Detection

Detects credit cards, passwords, Aadhaar, & human faces. Powered by YOLO WebGPU in milliseconds. Zero raw data ever leaves your device.
Privacy

🎨
Automatically blacks out private details on a hidden local canvas. Adds protective cushions around numbers while keeping buttons clickable.
AI

Action
Execution
🎯
Executes precise clicks, typing, & scrolling. Displays visual ripple rings on-screen. Asks user confirmation before financial actions.
